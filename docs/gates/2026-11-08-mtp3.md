## Why this candidate

Local A/B on **this pair and checkpoint but an earlier image**
(`a7f76ae9…`, not the deployed `3b5da79c…`), 2026-11-03: code decode medians
+5.31 to +11.89 %, no robust regression, 96 cells + 40 warmups per arm
(`glm53-fp4-local/local/ab-20261103/`). **That image difference is why this
window re-measures a matched MTP-5 control on the current build before the
change.** Independently of any image: Bizuayeu's ten-input sweep selects k=3
(k=5 loses prose; step time grows ~13.5 ms per draft depth) and msinacimen
measures MTP decode peaking at k=3 (295 → 310 tok/s from k=5 to k=3).
