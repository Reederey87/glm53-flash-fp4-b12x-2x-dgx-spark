## 3 — draft b12x

The [NVIDIA recipe](https://nvidia.github.io/local-ai-inference-recipes/hardware/dgx-spark-2gpu/index.html?backend=vllm&memory=128&memoryConfig=128&model=z-ai%2Fglm-5.3-flash&os=linux&precision=fp4-local)
uses Humming for the draft and b12x for the target. The current local
`vllm/vllm/config/speculative.py` explicitly describes unquantized drafts as a
reason to override the target MoE backend. Its unquantized oracle does not map
`b12x`; the deployed image's oracle has the same omission. This is evidence to
check actual draft quantization and backend dispatch, not proof of failure.
Newer [model-card launches](https://huggingface.co/nvidia/GLM-5.3-Flash-FP4-Local)
do specify b12x for the draft but change many other controls. Do not transplant
that complete launch into this window. FlashInfer's NVFP4 experts alone do not
establish support for this draft's representation.

The [autotrust draft](https://huggingface.co/autotrust/GLM5.3-Flash-E224-DGX-Spark/blob/main/MTP-NVFP4.md)
changes draft storage; it is a separate checkpoint/quantization experiment,
outside a simple backend substitution. The
[DevelopingDad patches](https://huggingface.co/DevelopingDad/GLM-5.3-Flash-FP4-Local-DGX-Spark-Optimized)
bundle scale buffering, adaptive MTP and checkpoint changes; their combined
result cannot be attributed to this candidate or assumed at 17 GiB KV.

## 4 — eight seats

[Engine arguments](https://docs.vllm.ai/en/latest/configuration/engine_args/)
describe max-num-seqs as runner/graph capacity, with a separate admission
limit in newer builds. Check whether the pinned build exposes it before use.
[PR38270](https://github.com/vllm-project/vllm/pull/38270) explains the
recurrent-cache-block floor and why shrinking capture sizes can hurt prefill.
The local speculator allocates max_num_reqs from max_num_seqs. More seats are
not free merely because their prompt tokens fit in the logical pool.

## 5 and 6 — image and FlashInfer

[Docker Hub's dated image](https://hub.docker.com/layers/eugr/spark-vllm-b12x/nightly-20261101/images/sha256-7d5ba755d5c69476e971feebaea73be74bc9b9e6f2f87573c61acd492c61e4f9)
reports ARM64 manifest `7d5ba755...`; resolve and retain image/config digests
before pulling or retagging. A registry manifest digest is not a Docker image ID.
[Upstream build documentation](https://github.com/eugr/spark-vllm-docker)
documents explicit FlashInfer ref/rebuild controls and separate experimental
wheel caches. Preserve the tested rollback image instead of overwriting its tag.
The local `91271757..361e45fa` b12x delta includes PCIe gather planning (the kit
uses RoCE), BF16 head64 decode and unweighted NVFP4 routes. No end-to-end GLM
gain follows from the commit count. A FlashInfer build must hold vLLM and all
other dependencies fixed, and verify its installed commit and adapter imports.
[Issue377](https://github.com/eugr/spark-vllm-docker/issues/377) illustrates why
loader changes must not be bundled into an image comparison.
